# ROOM//ZERO Pitch

Your Room is Not a Level. Your Room is the Game Engine.

## The Problem

Most VR games are beautiful and disconnected from real life. You put on a headset and leave your space. The desk, the table, the walls stop mattering.

## The Solution

ROOM//ZERO uses AI to scan the player's actual room and turn it into a fully playable heist world. Every surface becomes part of the game. Every session is different. ZERO learns how you play and writes the next mission for your style.

## What You Experience

1. Put on the headset. Say: "Give me a mission."
2. AI analyzes walls, desks, surfaces, open space.
3. A holographic heist appears using real furniture as the board.
4. Hands and voice only. No controllers.
5. Puzzles, security, choices.
6. ZERO remembers forever.
7. Tomorrow, same room, different game.

## Why This Wins

It is three winning ideas at once.

### Best Agentic AI

ZERO is not a chatbot. It is an orchestrator that decides missions, difficulty, NPC behavior, and world state. The AI creates.

### Boldest Original Concept

Your room is the game engine. Remove VR and the idea dies. It is fundamentally spatial. No other game can do this.

### Best Reason to Return

ZERO remembers you. Each session is different. You come back to beat your own record in a world that already knows you.

## How It Works

Six brain centers talk through structured language:

- ROOM SCOUT — where objects can appear, which surfaces are safe, where open space is
- GAME DIRECTOR — objective, length, difficulty
- PUZZLE ARCHITECT — puzzles that fit real geometry
- NPC AGENT — personality, memory, goals
- PLAYER MODEL — skill, speed, style, preferences
- ADAPTATION AGENT — help if stuck, raise difficulty if bored

No one agent controls everything. Organized chaos.
