# Three-Minute Video Hook

Total: 180 seconds. Real gameplay. Not a prototype slideshow.

| Time | Shot | Audio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-10s | Real room, no UI. Player puts on Quest 3. | "ZERO, give me a mission." |
| 10-25s | Passthrough. Holograms lock to desk and walls. Title card: ROOM//ZERO | Low drone + scan ticks |
| 25-60s | Hands. Code pad on real desk. Wire puzzle on shelf. | Diegetic beeps |
| 60-100s | NPC hologram on chair. Player speaks. NPC answers. | Voice in / voice out |
| 100-140s | Player stuck. Adaptation Agent drops a hint beam. Timer eases. | ZERO: "I moved the vent. Go." |
| 140-165s | Extraction. Vault opens on table. Mission complete. | Sting |
| 165-180s | Cut to same room, different player, different layout, different mission. | "Same room. New heist." |

Do not show desktops, engine views, or slides. Stay in headset.
